# PRODUCT.md — Furnace

## What this is

A single-page marketing site for **Furnace**, a (concept) workout-tracking app
for lifters who train for intensity. The app logs sets in two taps, computes
progressive-overload targets from your history, and runs strict rest timers.

## Audience

Serious and aspiring lifters, 18–40, who train alone — the garage gym at 11pm,
the commercial gym before dawn. People who respond to being challenged, not
coddled.

## The page's single job

Make the visitor *feel* the pull of effort — and want to train tonight.
The site itself behaves like a workout: scrolling it is doing it.

## Register

`brand` — design IS the product here. This is a campaign-grade landing page.

## Scene sentence (theme anchor)

A lifter alone in a garage gym at 11pm in winter, breath visible, phone propped
against a plate — the screen is the warmest thing in the room. → dark theme,
cold night-indigo world, effort rendered as molten heat.

## Voice

Second person, imperative, terse. The app talks like a training partner who
respects you too much to flatter you. Three brand-voice words:
**loaded, stubborn, incandescent.**
