Evidence & method

Award Audit is a detection tool, not payroll software and not legal advice. Findings indicate shifts that appear below award minimums and should be reviewed with a qualified adviser. Junior/apprentice rates, allowances, overtime amounts and enterprise agreements are out of scope of this check. Keeping a dated copy of this report supports the record-keeping expectations of the Voluntary Small Business Wage Compliance Code.
